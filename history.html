Royal Club: A History of Greatness

Foundation and Early Years

The football club Real Madrid (Real Madrid Club de Fútbol) was officially founded on March 6, 1902, by brothers Padrós and Julián Palacios. It was originally called Madrid Football Club.

By 1905, the club won its first official trophy — the Spanish Cup, defeating Athletic Bilbao in the final. In 1920, King Alfonso XIII of Spain granted the club the title "Royal" (Real), which is reflected in the modern name and the addition of the crown to the emblem.

The Santiago Bernabéu & Di Stéfano Era

The true golden age of the club began in the 1950s under the leadership of president Santiago Bernabéu. With the arrival of Alfredo Di Stéfano, Ferenc Puskás, and Francisco Gento, Real Madrid won the first five editions of the European Cup consecutively (1956–1960).

Best Club of the 20th Century

On December 11, 2000, the International Federation of Football History & Statistics (FIFA) recognized Real Madrid as the best football club of the 20th century. The team received a special trophy that is kept in the stadium's museum.
